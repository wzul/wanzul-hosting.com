About Wanzul Hosting

Wanzul Hosting started on 29 March 2014 to provide web hosting services. We began by subscribing to a reseller hosting service and reselling it with cheaper packages, and also sold domain names.

In 2018 we decided to stop taking new web hosting account registrations so we could focus on development. To this day, we have a wide array of expertise across various technology stacks.

We are currently building jomform.com, a platform where people can create forms and receive payments easily.

Services

1 Consultancy

Expert advice on web development, deployment workflows, and server tuning. Architecture, performance, and operations guidance. Experience in WordPress, Laravel and Ruby on Rails.

2 WordPress plugin development

Custom WordPress plugins built to your requirements: features, integrations, and maintenance. From small utilities to full product plugins.

3 WordPress deployment with High Availability (HA)

WordPress hosting and deployment designed for uptime and scale: redundant setups, staging, backups, and monitoring.

4 Email newsletter

Full setup and configuration for your email newsletters: installation, list management, templates, and delivery.
